# Limitations

This repo is a synthetic D6 calibration proof of concept for Pakistan SEZ reform work. It is not a production tax model, legal opinion, validated fiscal estimate, final incentive design, or pilot-selection tool.

The default public demo uses fully synthetic hypothetical-zone and enterprise records. These are not real BOI, SEZA, FBR, Finance, legal, developer, or enterprise records.

Outputs are provisional and require human review. The tool does not approve incentives, set final tax rates, determine final validated fiscal cost, select a pilot zone, or replace BOI, FBR, Finance Division, SEZA, Law Division, IMF, programme, fiscal modeller, legal counsel, or human review.

Real policy use requires:

- D4 legal review of legal authority, existing commitments, grandfathering, sunset, compensation, arbitration, and transition constraints.
- D5/FBR/Finance validation of tax paid, CIT foregone, customs exemptions, incentive utilization, fiscal envelope, and scaling assumptions.
- Enterprise-level BOI/SEZA/FBR evidence for production, construction, employment, exports, investment, CAPEX, R&D, training, and compliance.
- Additionality, counterfactual, displacement, and net fiscal/economic impact assessment.
- KPI assurance and audit-trigger design before any pilot or rollout.

Important modelling limits:

- Synthetic additionality cases are structural sensitivity assumptions, not causal econometric estimates.
- Reported production or construction is not proof of incentive effectiveness.
- The illustrative D5 fiscal envelope is not a validated fiscal ceiling.
- Administrative costs are capacity assumptions, not validated institutional estimates.
- The feasible frontier tests parameter combinations; it does not optimize or recommend a final rate, cap, threshold, instrument, or pilot.
- Any cost-based support language means temporary transition support only; all SEZ fiscal incentives phase out by 30 June 2035.
- Scenario Settings remain hidden from the default demo path unless `SHOW_ADVANCED_SCENARIOS=1`.
- Market distortion analysis, institutional role mapping, legislative sequencing, milestone mapping, and national-scale fiscal modelling remain future work.

The older source-derived structured screening dataset covers 35 detected zone profile records and 35 indicator records based on the source digest. Exact row-level verification for any source-derived/internal use should rely on the original workbook and source documents before policy use.
