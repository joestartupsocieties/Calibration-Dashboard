# Demo Decision Trace

## Decision Question

For one synthetic enterprise archetype or portfolio slice, what temporary cost-based incentive parameter combinations could be tested within an illustrative D5 fiscal envelope under low/base/high additionality assumptions?

## Trace

1. **Synthetic model-ready records**
   - The model first checks whether enterprise financial evidence is complete enough for calculation.
   - This is not support eligibility.

2. **Policy support status**
   - Legal, fiscal, compliance, and additionality gates determine whether a synthetic enterprise can be shown as a cost-based review input.
   - Non-compliant records can still be model-ready for accelerated-removal calculations.

3. **Reference path**
   - The no-SEZ reference applies ordinary CIT and receives no incentive-caused incremental income.

4. **Scenario comparison**
   - Status quo reports protected treatment through the 2035 cap.
   - Accelerated removal changes treatment for non-compliant records.
   - Cost-based regime applies ordinary CIT and transparent CAPEX/R&D/training deductions.
   - Combined transition/pilot blends non-pilot and pilot treatment by uptake share.

5. **Parameter controls**
   - Instrument package determines the eligible deduction instruments.
   - CAPEX rate changes the immediate-expensing deduction.
   - Annual cap limits generated deductions.
   - Threshold blocks claims below minimum annual qualifying expenditure.
   - Utilization controls how much generated deduction is claimed.
   - Uptake controls pilot participation share in the frontier.

6. **Fiscal envelope**
   - The envelope is an illustrative synthetic D5 ceiling.
   - The model reports tested fiscal cost, margin, and whether each combination is within the envelope.

7. **Additionality**
   - Low/base/high cases are sensitivity assumptions.
   - Observed activity is not treated as proof of incentive effectiveness.

8. **Verification**
   - Audited asset registers, invoices, payment evidence, in-zone use, related-party checks, and FBR/customs cross-checks are required before policy use.

9. **D7 handoff**
   - Pilot rules include triggers to continue, tighten, suspend, recalibrate, or terminate based on fiscal cost, additionality, claim disallowance, backlog, uptake, and compliance.

## Correct Demo Close

The output is a provisional parameter range to test or a precise blocker with missing field, owner, and analytical consequence. It is not a final rate, fiscal estimate, incentive approval, legal determination, or pilot selection.
